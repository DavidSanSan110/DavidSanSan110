## 👋 About

I'm a 24-year-old backend and platform engineer at **Telefónica**, where I build and operate microservices and the delivery platform around them. I care about **architecture that stays maintainable** — reusable service templates, CI/CD pipelines that cut build times, and GitOps delivery with ArgoCD and Helm.

I'm also deeply into **LLM systems**: multi-provider model clients, RAG, fine-tuning, and observability. I contribute upstream when I can, most recently to the **LangChain** Perplexity integration.
